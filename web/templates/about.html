About Mindloop

Mindloop is designed to bring clarity to your chaotic digital life. By simplifying how you track your habits, focus, and intentions, we help you close the loops in your mind.

Why Mindloop?

As a developer with attention challenges, Mindloop started as a personal CLI tool to manage my daily work routine. It began simply: Intents to set a single focus for the day, and Focus Sessions to break that intention into chunks of high-quality deep work.

This workflow proved effective. Soon, Habits and Journaling were added to close the loop on daily routines. Now, I start my day ticking off habits and end it with a mini-journal reflection—allowing me to truly disconnect, end work, and go touch some grass.

Core Features

Intents

Set one single, clear intention to track and focus on for the day. Cut out the noise and prioritize what matters most.

Focus Sessions

Break down your intents into manageable chunks of deep work. Time-boxed frames to maximize your productivity.

Habits

Build consistency with daily and weekly habit tracking. A feedback loop of positive reinforcement.

Journal & Notes

End your day with reflections to close the cognitive loop, and capture markdown notes on the fly.

Summary

High-level metrics and a bird's-eye view of your productivity data and habits.

The Void

A dedicated space with nothingness to sit back, breathe, and enjoy the moment.

Roadmap & Vision

  • Phase 1: Local Foundation

    CLI + Local SQLite + Core Features (Intents, Focus, Habits, Journal).

  • Phase 2: Web Interface

    Responsive UI, Markdown Notes, Themes, and Visual Data Representation.

  • Phase 3: Workflows & Integration (Upcoming)

    Tasks/Sub-tasks, Routines/Rituals, and Extended Auth Models.

Built with Go (Backend) & Pure HTML/CSS (Frontend).
